# Sound credits

`kc1000/` — "Keyboard Soundpack #1 [Typing and Single Keystrokes]" by unicaegames
(Cherry KC 1000, recorded with a Shure SM7B), CC0.
https://opengameart.org/content/keyboard-soundpack-1-typing-and-single-keystrokes

`keypress-020.wav` from the original pack is omitted (near-silent take).

`hermes/` — Hermes Precisa 305 recordings by Joseph SARDIN / BigSoundBank, CC0.
https://bigsoundbank.com/typewriter-key-s2842.html
https://bigsoundbank.com/machine-a-ecrire-espace-s2843.html

Original WAVs (#2842 key, #2843 space) converted from 48 kHz / 24-bit to
44.1 kHz / 16-bit mono with `afconvert`, then trimmed around the strike and
decay (2 ms pre-roll, 5 ms tail). Enter is derived from the key recording
at playback time; it is not a separate recorded carriage return.
